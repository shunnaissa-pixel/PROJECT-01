BUGANDO MEDICAL CENTER

Bugando Medical Center is a refferal,consultant and university teaching hosptal for the Lake and Western zones of the United Republic of Tanzania.It is situated along the shores of Lake Victoria in Mwanza City.It has over 950 beds and over 1300 employees.
